## Guidelines

A sidebar is a vertical panel of navigation down the side of an app.

**Use it** for apps with many sections that users move between often.

| Variant | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| Default | Full sidebar with icons and labels. |
| Collapses To Icons | Giving more room to content. Show labels as tooltips. |
| Floating | A sidebar that sits above the page with its own edge and shadow. |
| Inset | The main content sits in a framed panel beside the sidebar. |
| Right Side | Secondary panels, such as details or filters. |
| Groups And Badges | Many links in labelled groups, with counts such as unread items. |
| Loading | Links are still loading. Uses Skeleton. |

**Do**

- Highlight the current page.
- Keep group labels short and few.
- Let users collapse it, and remember their choice.

**Don't**

- Put more than about 15 links at the top level.
- Use badges for anything other than counts or status.
