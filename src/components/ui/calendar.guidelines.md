## Guidelines

A calendar shows a month grid so users can pick a date or a range of dates.

**Use it** inline on the page when the date is the main task, such as booking. In a form, use Date Picker instead, as it takes less space.

| Variant | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| Single | One date. |
| Range | A start and end date, such as a stay or a leave request. |
| Disabled Days | Block dates that can't be picked, such as past dates or full days. |
| Month And Year Dropdowns | Dates far from today, such as a date of birth. |

**Do**

- Open on the most likely month, often today.
- Explain why days are blocked, if it isn't obvious.
- Make sure arrow keys move between days.

**Don't**

- Make users click month by month to reach a distant year.
- Show blocked days with colour alone.
